HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

WASD / Arrow Keys — Move
E — Interact / Talk
Z / C — Rotate Camera
I — Inventory
Q — Quest Journal
V — Evidence Board
Space — Eye of Truth
M — Toggle Sound
ESC — Menu

TIPS

Press E near villagers and objects to investigate.
Collect evidence — the right proof unlocks new dialogue.
Eye of Truth (Space) reveals hidden evidence within range.
Higher Insight unlocks special NPCs and secrets.
Merchants (E) sell weapons, armor and potions. Earn gold from combat.
Select 2 evidence on the board and FORM THEORY for rewards.
Not everyone tells the truth. Compare stories, find the cracks.
Gain Skill Points on level-up and spend them in FEATS > SKILLS.
Investigate the village Bounty Board (near the guard) to accept hunts.
Hunt enemies for materials (fang/silk/shards) and craft at the Forge (⚒️).
Time passes — night enemies are +15% stronger. Rest at the Inn (🛏️) or via Menu for 10g.
From PAUSED, toggle Screen Chroma or Large Text for comfort.
On touch screens, use the on-screen D-pad and buttons.

SHOP

Gold: 0
Buy gear and supplies with gold. Click your items on the right to sell.

FEATS & STATS

BOUNTY BOARD

Hunt the marked enemies. Accept a contract, defeat the target, then claim your reward.

FORGE

Craft gear and potions from hunt materials. Visit the anvil (⚒️) or open via menu.
